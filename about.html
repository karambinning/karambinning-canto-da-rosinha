About Canto Da Rosinha

A café for the long lunch.

Canto Da Rosinha is the kind of café you fall into the habit of — not a destination, a routine. We serve Portuguese and Brazilian food the way it’s served at home, in a small room behind Lidl on Norfolk Street.

The kitchen

What we cook, and how.

The food is plain Portuguese and Brazilian café fare — pastéis de nata baked through the day, frango from the grill, bifanas and pork sandwiches at lunchtime, cakes the way our mothers made them, and a strong coffee that does what coffee is supposed to do.

We try not to fuss. The menu is short on purpose. The ingredients we use are the ones we can stand behind, and the things we don’t make well, we don’t put on the board.

A small shelf at the side holds the things we miss from back home — biscuits, conservas, cheeses, the cleaning powder you can’t get in the supermarket. Take a basket. We’re a café and a small mercearia at the same time.

The room
— azulejo

Where to find us — and what it’s like.

We’re on Norfolk Street, set back behind the Lidl car park. You’ll see the sign from the pavement. Inside it’s small and warm, a handful of tables, kids on the corner banquette, the radio in Portuguese or Brazilian Portuguese depending on the day.

Walk-in, family-friendly, casual. No bookings. Bring whoever you like.

“A small room, a strong coffee, a pastéis de nata that tastes like Lisbon.”
Three things we believe

A café is for the people who come back.

Short menu, done right.

We cook what we know how to cook. The pastry list is the pastry list. The grill is the grill. If something isn’t on the board, it’s because we don’t want to half-do it.

Speak how you speak.

Most of our regulars speak Portuguese or Brazilian Portuguese; plenty don’t. We meet you in whichever. A café is a place where you don’t need to translate to feel welcome.

Family-friendly, always.

Pushchairs come in, kids run around, grandparents stay an hour. That isn’t a tolerated inconvenience — it’s the actual job. A café for the long lunch is for everyone in the family.

Come in. Sit down. Stay an hour.
