Before you come

A couple of small things, just so you know.

No bookings.

We’re a walk-in café. Tables turn through the day, but at busy lunchtimes there may be a five-minute wait — have a coffee while you stand.

Pre-order pastéis.

For a box of pastéis or a whole grilled chicken, ring through. We’ll have it ready for you at the time you say. Less queueing, less waste.

Kids welcome, always.

Pushchairs fit. There’s a corner banquette for the kids. The pão de queijo is a very effective bribe.
